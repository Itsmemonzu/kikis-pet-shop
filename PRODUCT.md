# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

WordPress + Elementor (free) is the target. No WordPress install exists locally yet, so the first build is a static HTML/CSS/vanilla-JS prototype served on localhost, structured section-by-section as Elementor containers and free widgets (Heading, Text Editor, Image, Button, Icon Box, Icon List, Google Maps, HTML) with design tokens mirroring Elementor Global Colors / Global Fonts, so it can be rebuilt 1:1 through the Elementor MCP later. Elementor Pro is not assumed.

## Users

- Pet owners in Dhaka, mostly 30+ years old, looking for cat food, litter and accessories near Kallyanpur / Mirpur Road. They want to know quickly: what's in stock, where the shop is, how to order.
- The shop owners, who will maintain the site in Elementor themselves: structure must stay simple and editable.

## Product Purpose

A small brick-and-mortar pet shop's first website. It turns Facebook followers and local searchers into WhatsApp/Messenger orders and shop visits. Success = people message or visit.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood pet shop inside Khaja Supermarket that stocks the everyday essentials (wet food pouches, dry food, litter, accessories) and answers on WhatsApp. "Your pet's one-stop shop" / "পোষা প্রাণীর সবকিছুই এক ঠিকানায়".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Shop (category browsing, no cart), About, Contact. The site is cat-only; the dog category was removed on request (2026-11-23).
- Language: English with Bangla lines (taglines, key labels).
- Smooth animations and seamless page transitions are a hard requirement; must degrade cleanly under reduced motion.
- Undecided: exact product list and prices, opening hours, delivery area/charges.

- Logo: yellow paw print with a blue line-drawn dog and cat in the main pad.
- Existing cover uses navy blue wordmark, magenta/pink "PET SHOP", Bangla tagline.
- Taglines: "Your pet's one-stop shop" and "পোষা প্রাণীর সবকিছুই এক ঠিকানায়".

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo, cover photo and product post photos from the Facebook page (downloaded to `assets/fb/`).
- Recent post: wet food pouches available.
- 60 followers, 0 reviews. No testimonials, ratings, prices, brand partnerships or delivery promises exist: do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Ordering is one tap away on every screen (WhatsApp first).
2. Plain, large, readable: built for 30+ eyes and thumbs.
3. Real shop, real address: being findable beats being clever.
4. Owner-editable: every section maps to a stock Elementor widget.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Base body text ≥ 18px, generous line height, high contrast, large tap targets (≥ 48px), no hover-only information, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
