## Scope

Kiki's Pet Shop marketing site: Home (landing), Shop, About, Contact. Visitor mode: Persuade. Static prototype (site/) structured for 1:1 rebuild in Elementor free via MCP.

## Audience, action, proof

- Dhaka pet owners, 30+, mostly on phones, near Kallyanpur / Mirpur Road. Cat-only (shop's own "meow-baby" voice); dog category removed on request.
- Action: order on WhatsApp (01680042538) first; Messenger second; visit Shop 122 (map) third.
- Proof: real shelf photos (Snacky, Wanpy, Paw Paw, Lola & Co, Bellotta cans), the in-store tour reel, the real address. No prices, reviews, delivery promises or hours beyond what Facebook states.

## Direction contract

Redesign pinned by the user (2026-11-23): "modernize it, the blue is too contrasty, more modern, minimal, more pet shop vibe with the pics I give you". The four user photos (golden-hour cats: blinds nap, golden profile, persian glow, sun in grass) set the world. The user's pin beats the roll; the navy directory-board world is retired.

THESIS: A calm, sunlit neighbourhood pet shop: the page feels like the warm light in the photos, and ordering is one honey-coloured button away. It refuses both the navy signboard it replaces and the pastel paw-confetti pet template.

OWN-WORLD: Clean white page ground (#ffffff; amended from warm paper per the user's "modern, minimal" and "looks too ai") with warm stone sections (#f5f0ea) and hairline dividers (#e6dccf); warm near-black ink (#2b2420) and muted cocoa text (#6b5f55); one honey accent from the photos' light (#f2b13d) for action and "you are here" only (never as caption or label fills), deep amber (#a35d0c) for accent text on light. Cocoa footer only. Anek Bangla at normal width, 700 for headings, generous size, tight tracking. Photos carry the colour; the interface stays quiet. Rounded 14px photos, 10px controls, flat (no shadows except the mobile dock).

STORY: A visitor meets a sunlit cat and a plain promise (cat food, litter and treats in Kallyanpur), sees exactly where the shop is, taps the honey WhatsApp button, or scans the category list and real shelf photos first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light header with logo, bilingual wordmark, text nav (active link in a honey pill) and a dark WhatsApp button. Hero on paper: left column holds the 2F / 122 / Khaja chips, the H1, the Bangla tagline, the honey WhatsApp action with the number, the Messenger link, then the five category rows in a white panel; right column holds the blinds-nap photo, tall and rounded, with a "Watch the shop tour" chip. On mobile: chips, H1, Bangla, WhatsApp, rows, photo.

FORM: User-pinned "golden hour shop" re-skin; no roll (pinned direction beats the roll). seed: none (roll skipped: direction pinned by the user). Previous reference seed:926c5a2cae4e1bf9 retired with the old world. Imagery: the four user photos plus the shop's own shelf photos lead; daylight stock appears only second to a real shelf photo on the Shop page. Signature interaction kept: the honey "you are here" pill slides between nav links on page change (cross-document View Transitions) while content rises in; the floor line (Bus stand → Khaja Supermarket → 2nd floor → Shop 122) draws itself on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
