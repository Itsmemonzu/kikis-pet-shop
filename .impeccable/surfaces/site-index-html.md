## Scope

Kiki's Pet Shop marketing site: Home (landing), Shop, About, Contact. Visitor mode: Persuade. Static prototype (site/) structured for 1:1 rebuild in Elementor free via MCP.

## Audience, action, proof

- Dhaka pet owners, 30+, mostly on phones, near Kallyanpur / Mirpur Road. Cat-only (shop's own "meow-baby" voice); dog category removed on request.
- Action: order on WhatsApp (01680042538) first; Messenger second; visit Shop 122 (map) third.
- Proof: real shelf photos (Snacky, Wanpy, Paw Paw, Lola & Co, Bellotta cans), the in-store tour reel, the real address. No prices, reviews, delivery promises or hours beyond what Facebook states.

## Direction contract

Redesign pinned by the user (2026-11-23): "modernize it, the blue is too contrasty, more modern, minimal, more pet shop vibe with the pics I give you". The four user photos (golden-hour cats: blinds nap, golden profile, persian glow, sun in grass) set the world. The user's pin beats the roll; the navy directory-board world is retired.

THESIS: A calm neighbourhood cat shop in its own colours: frosted glass floats over warm cream light and the owner's sunlit photos, and ordering is one tap away. It refuses the sticker-and-hanging-tag layout that copied suirenbd.com and the pastel paw-confetti pet template.

OWN-WORLD: The client palette: navy #27307A (headings, dark buttons, the hero WhatsApp action, footer), gold #D7B35C / lit #E4C77F for action and "you are here" only, always with ink text; cream #F3E7C8 page ground with light cream #FBF5E6, text #1D2352, muted #4C517A, hairline rgb(39 48 122 / .14). The page ground carries fixed soft gold and navy radial light so glass has something to blur. Glass: warm white rgb(255 251 240 / .58), or .78 over photos, white edge rgb(255 255 255 / .7), blur 18px saturate 1.35, inset top highlight and one soft navy lift (0 22px 50px -32px rgb(29 35 82 / .55)), on the header, hero card, pills, shelf case, order panel, lists, page heads and notes. Anek Bangla only (700 headings, 500 Bangla, 400 body). Radii 14 / 18 / 22 / 26 / 28-36 and 999 pills. Everything straight: no tilts, stickers, hanging tags, stamp or footer wordmark.

STORY: A visitor meets a sunlit cat and a plain promise (cat food, litter and treats in Kallyanpur), sees exactly where the shop is, taps the navy WhatsApp button, or scans the category list and real shelf photos first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A floating glass header bar (logo plate, bilingual wordmark, pill nav with the gold "you are here" pill, navy WhatsApp button). Hero is a full photo stage (blinds-nap, 36px radius, up to 76svh) with a frosted card bottom-left holding the 2F / 122 / Khaja chips, the H1, the Bangla tagline, the navy WhatsApp action with the number and the Messenger link, plus a frosted "Watch the shop tour" tag top-right. On mobile the photo becomes a 4:3.6 image and the glass card overlaps its bottom edge; glass category pills follow.

FORM: User-directed redesign (2026-11-23): the client's navy, gold and cream plus glass site-wide, replacing the sticker layout. seed: none (roll skipped: direction pinned by the user). Imagery: the four user photos plus the shop's own shelf photos lead. Motion unchanged: the photo settles and the card rises on first view, scroll reveals, the floor line (Bus stand -> Khaja Supermarket -> 2nd floor -> Shop 122) draws itself, the gold pill slides between nav links on page change (cross-document View Transitions), and reduced motion turns it all off.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
